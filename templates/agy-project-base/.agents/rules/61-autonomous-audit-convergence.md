# Autonomous Product Convergence

The runtime records iterations only for diagnostics. Routine correction continues while progress is measurable and never requires owner approval merely because a counter changed.

Continue automatically while at least one material fact changes: an open product blocker is narrowed or closed, a failing deterministic check changes, a candidate file identity changes in the intended scope, or a required artifact becomes valid.

Stop after two consecutive identical/no-progress outcomes, or when a true owner decision is required. A late material finding is registered and repaired within the same work item; it does not consume a quota.

The initial comprehensive audit must cover every dimension declared by the work item. Malformed findings, incomplete coverage and stale candidate manifests fail closed and are corrected by the control plane before product closure.
